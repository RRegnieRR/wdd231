NP Membership Benefits

This level has no fee and includes member directory listing, networking events, and community updates.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Bronze members receive all NP benefits plus business training sessions and event discounts.

Silver Membership Benefits

Silver members receive Bronze benefits plus extra advertising and possible spotlight placement.

Gold Membership Benefits

Gold members receive Silver benefits plus premium spotlight positions, special event invitations, and priority training access.
